Our Lessons

A Journey of Grace

Our lessons are designed to introduce students to the foundations of Indian traditional dance while gradually developing rhythm, expression, posture and confidence.

01 — FOUNDATION

Dance Fundamentals

Learn basic postures, hand gestures, footwork, body movements and rhythm patterns.

02 — TECHNIQUE

Classical Technique

Develop precision and control through traditional dance movements and structured practice.

03 — EXPRESSION

Abhinaya

Explore facial expressions, storytelling and emotional expression through dance.

04 — RHYTHM

Tala & Rhythm

Understand rhythm patterns and coordinate movement with traditional musical structures.

05 — PRACTICE

Regular Practice

Build consistency and confidence through guided practice and repeated movement exercises.

06 — PERFORMANCE

Performance Training

Prepare for stage performances by developing presentation, confidence and expressive movement.
