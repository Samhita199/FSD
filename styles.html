Expressions of Tradition

Every Indian dance form has its own language of movement, rhythm, expression and storytelling.

01

Bharatanatyam

Rhythm • Expression • Grace

A celebrated classical dance form known for precise footwork, expressive gestures, graceful poses and powerful storytelling.

02

Kathak

Movement • Drama • Expression

A graceful dance tradition combining rhythmic movement, expressive storytelling and elements of classical theatre.

03

Kuchipudi

Rhythm • Spins • Storytelling

Recognised for intricate footwork, graceful turns, rhythmic patterns and expressive storytelling.

04

Odissi

Sculptural • Graceful • Expressive

A graceful classical form distinguished by fluid movements, sculptural poses and expressive interpretation.

05

Mohiniyattam

Softness • Beauty • Expression

Known for gentle swaying movements, graceful expressions and an elegant style of presentation.

06

Manipuri

Devotion • Fluidity • Grace

A delicate dance tradition featuring smooth movements, devotional themes and refined expressions.
